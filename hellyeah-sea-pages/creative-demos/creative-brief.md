# Hellyeah Creative Demo Brief

## Deliverables

- Four audience-specific concepts
- Meta Feed: 1080 x 1080 PNG
- Stories / Reels: 1080 x 1920 PNG
- Google / Performance Max landscape: 1200 x 628 PNG
- Twelve final image demos in total

## Shared Offer

- Free 14-day pilot
- $0 today
- No card
- Media spend excluded
- The user reviews the campaign before any launch decision

## Audience Concepts

### Owner-led Businesses

- Tension: advertising feels like learning another job
- Promise: add the website and goal; review the assembled campaign
- CTA: Build my first campaign

### Ecommerce Operators

- Tension: the product and offer are ready before the campaign is
- Promise: turn one product page into a focused sales campaign
- CTA: Launch my product campaign

### In-house Marketers

- Tension: an approved brief still spends a week in campaign setup
- Promise: get audience, first-draft ads and budget ready for team review
- CTA: Turn my brief into a campaign

### Small Agencies

- Tension: signed briefs enter another repetitive setup queue
- Promise: increase delivery capacity while preserving agency and client approval
- CTA: Build my next client campaign

## Production Note

The demo images use the licensed photography already used by the four landing pages. All offer text and CTA typography is rendered deterministically in HTML before raster export, so the final PNGs do not contain AI-generated spelling errors.
